# Product-Market Fit

Skills for the stage where you are proving who to sell to and why they buy. PMF is measured by customer value realization and retention, not revenue.

The intended sequence: build ground truth first (`customer-dossier`), then derive who to sell to (`icp-agent`), then pressure-test the message (`icp-objection-mapping`), and mine your calls for what customers actually say (`conversational-intelligence`, `blueprint-swarm`).

| Skill | What it does | Status |
|---|---|---|
| [`customer-dossier`](./customer-dossier) | Builds a ground-truth customer profile per account from CRM, calls, billing, product, and support data: one timeline, provenance on every field, conflicts surfaced. The foundation for any ICP analysis | Available |
| [`icp-agent`](./icp-agent) | Builds a Red/Yellow/Green ICP definition with a weighted scoring model, from a quick-start interview or a CRM deep dive | Available |
| [`icp-objection-mapping`](./icp-objection-mapping) | Role-plays your skeptical ICP before you write campaigns, so objections get preempted in the copy instead of discovered in replies | Available |
| [`conversational-intelligence`](./conversational-intelligence) | Extracts structured intelligence from call transcripts (handoff context, competitive intel, expansion signals, closed-lost analysis) with verbatim-quote discipline | Available |
| [`blueprint-swarm`](./blueprint-swarm) | Analyzes hundreds of call transcripts and CRM records in parallel sub-agent waves, with an adversarial auditor that kills hallucinated findings. Based on Jordan Crawford's open-source blueprint-swarm | Available |
