# GTM Readiness Deep Scan: Questionnaire v1.0

Part of the `gtm-readiness-scan` skill (Deep Scan mode). The Quick Scan (24 questions, self-serve) lives in `questionnaire.md`; this file is the full 120-question interview-led assessment: 8 domains, 5 questions per subcategory, 6 answer anchors per question scored 0-5, with evidence prompts per question. A hosted, AI-coached version of this assessment lives at https://www.gtmscan.app.


# GTM Readiness Deep Scan: Questionnaire v1.0

## Instructions for Assessor

**Delivery model:** 4 structured interview sessions (90 min each) + CRM/data review + scoring workshop.

**Per interview session:**
1. Cover 2 domains (see interview schedule below)
2. Read each question; show answer options
3. Participants select the answer that **best describes their organization today**
4. Capture evidence/rationale for each answer (documents, dashboards, examples)
5. Flag questions where participants disagree by 2+ points
6. Note any "we don't know" answers, these default to score 0

**Scoring:** Each question scored by the most relevant perspective(s). Subcategory score = average of 5 questions. Domain score = average of 3 subcategories. Overall index = average of 8 domains.

**Interview schedule:**

| Session | Domains | Participants | Duration |
|---------|---------|-------------|----------|
| 1 | D1 (ICP & Market) + D2 (Sales Process) | CRO/VP Sales, Head of Marketing, Sales Ops, Rev Ops | 90 min |
| 2 | D3 (Messaging & Positioning) + D4 (Pipeline & Revenue) | CMO, Head of Demand Gen, Content Lead, Sales Ops | 90 min |
| 3 | D5 (Outbound & Prospecting) + D6 (Tech Stack & Ops) | SDR Manager, Sales Ops, Marketing Ops, Rev Ops | 90 min |
| 4 | D7 (Team & Enablement) + D8 (Customer Expansion) | VP Sales, Enablement Lead, CS Lead, HR/L&D | 90 min |

| **1** | Ad-hoc | Some individuals do this informally. No organizational approach. |
| **2** | Developing | Initial efforts exist, but they are inconsistent, undocumented, or fragmented. |
| **3** | Defined | A standardized approach is documented, communicated, and applied across the team. |
| **4** | Managed | The capability is measured, monitored, and systematically improved. Data drives decisions. |
| **5** | Optimized | Continuously improving, best-in-class, and a source of competitive advantage. |

## Calculating Scores

**Subcategory score** = Average of 5 questions in that subcategory (0.0-5.0)
**Domain score** = Average of 3 subcategory scores within the domain (0.0-5.0)
**Overall GTM Readiness Index** = Average of all 8 domain scores (0.0-5.0)

## Interpreting Domain Scores

| Score Range | Interpretation | Recommendation |
|-------------|---------------|----------------|
| 0.0-1.0 | **Critical gap**, This domain is a significant risk to GTM success | Immediate attention required. Foundational work needed before scaling. |
| 1.1-2.0 | **Early stage**, Some awareness but no systematic capability | Prioritize building the basics. Document, standardize, and assign ownership. |
| 2.1-3.0 | **Developing**, Foundations are in place but inconsistent | Focus on consistency, documentation, and cross-functional alignment. |
| 3.1-4.0 | **Solid**, Defined capabilities with room for optimization | Shift from building to measuring and improving. Invest in data-driven refinement. |
| 4.1-5.0 | **Advanced**, Mature, measured, and continuously improving | Maintain and extend advantage. Focus on compounding intelligence and innovation. |

## Interpreting Overall GTM Readiness Index

| Index | GTM Readiness Level | Implication |
|-------|---------------------|-------------|
| 0.0-1.5 | **Not ready to scale** | Fundamental GTM capabilities are missing. Scaling now would burn cash without results. Focus on foundations. |
| 1.6-2.5 | **Foundation building** | Core elements are emerging. Continue building before investing heavily in growth. Prioritize domains with lowest scores. |
| 2.6-3.5 | **Ready for controlled growth** | The GTM engine is functional. Growth is possible but requires careful management. Optimize weak domains while growing. |
| 3.6-4.5 | **Scalable** | A strong GTM foundation supports confident scaling. Focus on efficiency, intelligence, and compounding advantages. |
| 4.6-5.0 | **Best-in-class** | A mature, data-driven GTM engine. Focus on maintaining edge, innovation, and expanding into new markets or segments. |

## Cross-Domain Analysis

After scoring all domains, look for these patterns:

1. **Execution without foundation** (Domains 6-7 score higher than Domains 1-4): The team is active but not strategically grounded. Risk of wasted effort and inconsistent results.
2. **Strategy without execution** (Domains 1-4 score higher than Domains 5-8): Good strategic thinking but poor operational capability. Invest in execution infrastructure.
3. **Tool-heavy, process-light** (Domain 5 scores high, Domain 7 scores low): Over-investment in technology without developing the people and processes to use it effectively.
4. **Person-dependent, system-weak** (Domain 7 scores high, Domain 8 scores low): Strong individuals but weak systems. Growth is constrained by key-person dependency.
5. **Outbound-dominant, inbound-absent** (6A scores high, 6C scores low): Over-reliance on outbound creates fragility. Invest in demand generation for long-term resilience.

## Usage Notes

- Score based on **current state**, not aspirations or plans
- When in doubt, score lower, optimism bias is the most common assessment error
- "We don't know" = score 0 (lack of visibility is itself a gap)
- Disagreement between participants (2+ points) signals a process or communication gap worth investigating
- Use evidence prompts to ground scores in verifiable facts, not opinions
