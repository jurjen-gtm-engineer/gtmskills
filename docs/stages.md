# Skills by growth stage

The right playbook depends on where you are. Run an assessment first, then work the stage it names.

## Not sure where you are: assessments

| Skill | Question it answers | What it needs |
|---|---|---|
| `gtm-readiness-scan` | How mature is our go-to-market across 8 domains? | 24 questions, or the full 120-question interview |
| `bowtie-benchmark` | How do our conversion rates compare to companies with our deal size? | Your funnel metrics |
| `crm-scorecard` | What do our conversion rates look like when computed from the CRM itself? | CRM export files, any CRM |
| `spiced-call-scorecard` | Do our calls capture what the customer needs in order to buy? | Call transcripts |
| `gtm-diagnostic` | What is the main cause holding growth back, and what do we do in 30, 60 and 90 days? | CRM data, calls and a few weeks |

## Product-market fit

You are here if customers renew and get value, but you are still proving who to sell to and why they buy.

Run in this order: `customer-dossier` (ground truth per account), `win-loss-rewind` when you have outcome data or `icp-agent` when you do not, `icp-objection-mapping` (pressure-test the message), then `conversational-intelligence` and `blueprint-swarm` to mine your calls.

## GTM fit

You are here if you have proof and now need a sales motion that repeats and pays for itself.

- **Before you spend on data:** `tam-data-cost-estimate`, `crm-enrichment-cost-estimate`, `free-first-domain-resolver`.
- **Segments and messages:** `playbook-generator`, `pqs-pvp-messaging`, `research-playbook`, `follow-up-sequences`, `qa-checklist`, and the Clay prompts.
- **Sales meetings:** `meeting-prep`, `battlecard-builder`.
- **Automation:** `gtm-ai-brief`, `claygent-prompt-generator`, `claygent-builder`.

## Growth and moat

You are here if the motion repeats and you now scale it, measure it and defend it.

`compound-growth-check` (is growth compounding or decaying), `outcome-seller-scoring` (what the coaching gap is worth), `ai-use-case-gate` (go or no-go on an AI build), `epistemic-context-grounding` and `context-gap-analysis` (make the AI check what it knows first).

Twenty-six small prompts for Clay columns and Claygent. Start with `prompt-engineering-rules` and `metaprompter`, then pick the research or scoring prompt you need. The full list is in the [README](../README.md#the-skills).
