# Existential Data Point (EDP) Analysis Framework

## Context

I have research data about the [INSERT CATEGORY]. I need you to analyze this data to determine the existential data point (EDP) for this category and identify pain-based segments.

An Existential Data Point is the critical metric or information that creates genuine urgency and transforms a nice-to-have solution into a must-have requirement. It's the data point so crucial to a business that it can determine success or failure. Unlike regular metrics that might be interesting but not actionable, existential data points create immediate tension and drive decision-making by highlighting critical vulnerabilities or opportunities that cannot be ignored.

## Instructions

### 1. Thoroughly analyze the provided research

Review the research on [INSERT CATEGORY NAME] considering industry trends, customer pain points, market dynamics, and competitive pressures.

### 2. Propose 3-5 potential Existential Data Points

For each potential EDP:

- Clearly define the metric/data point
- Explain why it creates genuine urgency
- Describe how it transforms the solution from nice-to-have to must-have
- Provide examples of how it manifests in real organizations
- Identify data sources that would help measure or validate this EDP

### 3. Evaluate each potential EDP

Rate each potential EDP against these criteria (scale 1-10 and explain your reasoning):

- **Urgency:** How quickly must a customer act on this data point?
- **Universality:** How broadly applicable is this across the category's customer base?
- **Measurability:** How easily can this data point be quantified and tracked?
- **Actionability:** How directly does this drive concrete decisions?
- **Defensibility:** How difficult is this for competitors to address or replicate?

### 4. Recommend the strongest EDP candidate

Based on your evaluation, provide a comprehensive explanation of why this should be the focal EDP for the category.

### 5. Develop 4-6 Pain-Based Segments

Based on your recommended EDP, create segments with:

- **Name:** Descriptive, memorable title for each segment
- **TAM Estimate:** Total Addressable Market for the segment
- **Pain Intensity:** Rate on a scale of 0-1
- **Conversion Rate Potential:** Estimated conversion percentage
- **ACV Range:** Annual Contract Value range
- **Sales Efficiency Factor:** Relative sales efficiency
- **Value ARR Estimate:** Calculated potential ARR
- **Overall Segment Score:** Out of 100

### 6. Create a visual representation

Develop a bullseye or similar metaphor showing which segments face the most acute pain relative to the EDP.

### 7. Provide strategic recommendations

Include:

- **Segment Prioritization:** Which segments to target first and why
- **Permissionless Value Proposition Ideas:** For each priority segment
- **Key Messaging Themes:** That would resonate with each segment
- **Data Collection Strategies:** To further validate the EDP and segments
